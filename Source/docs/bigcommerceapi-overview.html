Pay-by-card for BigCommerce shops

A plain-language overview of the integration between BigCommerce and WSPay.

What it is. An app that lets online shops running on BigCommerce accept credit-card payments through WSPay (a payment processor common in our region). It sits quietly between the shop and the card processor — the shop owner sets it up once, and from then on it just runs.

Who it's for

  • Online shop owners using BigCommerce who want to take card payments through WSPay.
  • Their customers, who want to pay by card without leaving the shop (or with a quick trip to a secure payment page).
  • The Monri team, who configure each shop and keep the integration healthy.

What a customer sees

  1. They fill their cart on the BigCommerce shop and get to the checkout page.
  2. Among the payment options, one says "Pay by card" (the exact label is set per shop).
  3. They pick it. One of two things happens, depending on how the shop is configured:
    • Embedded mode — a small card-entry form opens inside the same page. They type their card, pay, and never leave the shop.
    • Redirect mode — they click "Pay" and get sent to a WSPay-hosted page to enter their card, then bounced back to the shop's "order confirmed" screen.
  4. If they tick "save my card", next time they shop on the same store they can pick the saved card from a dropdown and skip re-entering it.
  5. If the payment succeeds, the BigCommerce order moves to Completed. If it fails, it goes to Cancelled. If the shop later refunds or partially refunds, that flows through too.

Who configures what

  • A shop owner — or a Monri admin on their behalf — sets up a store record in our admin panel: which BigCommerce shop, which WSPay merchant account, embedded vs redirect mode, what label to show on checkout, where to send the customer after payment.
  • Once it's set up, customers never see our app. They only see the BigCommerce shop and (sometimes briefly) a WSPay payment page.

What we don't do

  • We don't take the money. WSPay handles the actual card processing and bank settlement.
  • We don't store card numbers. When a customer saves a card, all we keep is a WSPay-issued token that means "this customer's saved card."
  • We don't run the shop. The product catalogue, shipping, taxes, emails — all of that stays in BigCommerce. We only handle the payment step.

When something goes wrong

  • A failed payment becomes a cancelled order in BigCommerce, automatically.
  • If WSPay can't reach us with the result, the order stays in its previous state until WSPay retries. We log every call so the team can debug.
  • Refunds and voids initiated from our admin tools flow back to BigCommerce so the shop owner's order list stays accurate.

In one sentence: the customer pays on the BigCommerce shop, WSPay processes the card, and our app keeps the two sides in sync.
